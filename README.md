# Vocably – Learn Vocabulary Smarter

Vocably is a clean and focused vocabulary learning app designed to help users improve their English through interactive practice and meaningful repetition.

It is ideal for students, professionals, and anyone who wants to strengthen their vocabulary in a simple, distraction-free way.

---

## 📱 App Features

- Definition Match – connect words with their meanings
- Synonym Match – learn similar-meaning words
- Antonym Match – understand contrasts clearly
- Semantic Grouping – strengthen word associations
- Quick Practice Sessions
- iPhone & iPad optimized layouts
- Offline-friendly learning

---

## 🎯 How Vocably Helps

- Builds vocabulary through active recall
- Reinforces meaning using relationships between words
- Encourages long-term retention
- No ads interrupting learning
- No account or sign-in required

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Data

Vocably respects your privacy.

- No account required
- No personal data collected
- No tracking or analytics
- No data shared with third parties

All learning progress is stored locally on your device.
