# Privacy Policy for Vocably

Last updated: January 2025

Vocably respects your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share personal data.

---

## Information Collection

Vocably does **not** collect:
- Personal information
- Names, email addresses, or identifiers
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos or media
- Usage analytics
- Advertising data

---

## Data Usage

- No data is transmitted off the device
- All learning progress is stored locally on the user’s device
- No third-party services are used for tracking or analytics

---

## Children’s Privacy

Vocably does not knowingly collect data from children.  
Because no data is collected, there is no risk of data misuse.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be posted on this page.
